# Tamkar — Product Launches Worth Noticing

Tamkar is a product-launch, discovery, and feedback community where founders share what they are building and thoughtful early users help them improve it.

Visit [tamkar.app](https://tamkar.app).

## How Tamkar works

1. **Share** — Add your product, stage, story, and an optional focused question.
2. **Discover** — Explore real products from independent founders and makers.
3. **Try** — Visit the product and understand what the founder is building.
4. **Respond** — Leave candid, useful feedback the founder can act on.
5. **Improve** — Founders reply, acknowledge feedback, and use it in the next version.

## What you can do

- Publish and manage a product launch
- Browse organic discovery and Community Favorites
- Ask a focused feedback question
- Leave general feedback or answer a founder's question
- Reply to feedback and continue the conversation
- Join community discussions and follow builders
- Save products to revisit later
- Apply for Tamkar Verified
- Use Founder AI to synthesize your own launch evidence
- Purchase a clearly labeled seven-day Sponsored Spotlight

## Trust principles

Tamkar keeps paid visibility separate from earned recognition. Sponsored Spotlight never buys votes, credibility, or Community Favorite status. Verified accounts are reviewed, and public launch eligibility is moderated.
